# 📘 Cunningham — Chapter to IB ESS Topic Map

**Book:** *Environmental Science: A Global Concern*, 16th Edition (2024)  
**Authors:** Cunningham, Cunningham, O'Reilly, Winsett — McGraw Hill  
**Syllabus:** IB ESS 2025+ (First Assessments 2025)

---

## 📌 How to Use This Note

Use the table below to find which Cunningham chapters support each IB ESS topic when planning lessons, building notes, or setting supplementary reading.

---

## 📊 Chapter → ESS Topic Map

| # | Chapter Title | IB ESS Topic(s) | Notes |
|---|---------------|-----------------|-------|
| Intro | Learning to Learn | — | Study skills / critical thinking primer |
| 1 | Understanding Our Environment | **T1** Foundations of ESS | Environmental history, sustainability, SDGs, ecological footprint, planetary boundaries, environmental justice |
| 2 | Principles of Science and Systems | **T1** Foundations of ESS | Systems thinking, feedback loops, scientific method, models — core to all of Topic 1 |
| 3 | Matter, Energy, and Life | **T2** Ecosystems | Energy flow, trophic levels, thermodynamics, photosynthesis/respiration, biogeochemical cycles (C, N, P, H₂O) |
| 4 | Evolution, Biological Communities & Species Interactions | **T2** Ecosystems · **T3** Biodiversity | Niches, predation, competition, symbiosis, keystone species, succession, resilience, tipping points |
| 5 | Biomes: Global Patterns of Life | **T2** Ecosystems | Terrestrial biomes, marine & freshwater ecosystems, human disturbance of biomes |
| 6 | Population Biology | **T2** Ecosystems · **T3** Biodiversity | Population dynamics, carrying capacity, r/K strategies — underpins population ecology and conservation |
| 7 | Human Populations | **T8** Human Systems & Resource Use | Population growth, demographic transition, age-structure diagrams, urbanisation drivers |
| 8 | Environmental Health & Toxicology | **T4** Water Systems · **T6** Atmosphere & Climate | Pollutant pathways, bioaccumulation, biomagnification, risk assessment |
| 9 | Food and Hunger | **T5** Soil & Terrestrial Food Production · **T4** Water Systems | Food security, caloric needs, aquaculture overview, food system inequities |
| 10 | Farming: Conventional & Sustainable Practices | **T5** Soil & Terrestrial Food Production | Green revolution, soil degradation, organic farming, GMOs, agroecology, food waste |
| 11 | Biodiversity: Preserving Species | **T3** Biodiversity & Conservation | Species richness, IUCN categories, extinction drivers, in-situ & ex-situ conservation, CITES |
| 12 | Biodiversity: Preserving Landscapes | **T3** Biodiversity & Conservation | Habitat fragmentation, protected areas, wildlife corridors, conservation strategies |
| 13 | Restoration Ecology | **T3** Biodiversity & Conservation | Ecological restoration, rewilding, rehabilitation — links to HL rewilding content |
| 14 | Geology & Earth Resources | **T8** Human Systems & Resource Use | Mineral extraction, non-renewable resources, e-waste, circular economy |
| 15 | Climate Systems & Climate Change | **T6** Atmosphere & Climate | Greenhouse effect, climate feedbacks, IPCC projections, tipping points, mitigation vs adaptation |
| 16 | Air Pollution | **T6** Atmosphere & Climate | Primary/secondary pollutants, photochemical smog, acid deposition, ozone depletion, indoor air quality |
| 17 | Water Use & Management | **T4** Water Systems | Freshwater scarcity, dams, irrigation, groundwater depletion, water governance |
| 18 | Water Pollution | **T4** Water Systems | Point vs non-point sources, eutrophication, sewage treatment, ocean plastic |
| 19 | Conventional Energy | **T7** Energy, Industry & Society | Fossil fuels, nuclear power, EROI, energy security, environmental impacts |
| 20 | Sustainable Energy | **T7** Energy, Industry & Society | Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, bioenergy — technology, limitations, storage challenges |
| 21 | Solid, Toxic & Hazardous Waste | **T8** Human Systems & Resource Use | Landfills, incineration, recycling, e-waste, plastic pollution, circular economy |
| 22 | Urbanisation & Sustainable Cities | **T8** Human Systems & Resource Use | Urban growth, ecological footprint of cities, green infrastructure, sustainable urban planning |
| 23 | Ecological Economics | **T1** Foundations of ESS · **T8** Human Systems | Natural capital, ecosystem services valuation, cost-benefit analysis, degrowth, green economy |
| 24 | Environmental Policy, Law & Planning | **T8** Human Systems & Resource Use | MEAs (Kyoto, Paris, CBD), national policy, EIA, Polluter Pays Principle, international governance |
| 25 | What Then Shall We Do? | **T1** Foundations of ESS | Personal and collective action, environmental worldviews, values — great for EVS discussions |
