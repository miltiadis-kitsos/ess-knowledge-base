## 🔑 Key Cross-Links

**Values → EVS:**
[[1.1.3 Defining values]] defines the building blocks that feed into [[1.1.7 Environmental Value Systems (EVS)]] as inputs.

**Worldviews → EVS categories:**
[[1.1.6 Worldviews]] (cultural/religious lenses) explains *why* the three positions in [[1.1.8 Technocentric, Anthropocentric and Ecocentric Worldviews]] exist.

**Showing values vs. surveying them:**
[[1.1.4 How Values Are Shown]] (values visible through action) and [[1.1.5 Values Surveys]] (values measured through research) are two complementary ways of externalising values described in [[1.1.3 Defining values]].

**Change over time → Environmental movement:**
[[1.1.9 Change in Perspectives Over Time]] explains the *mechanism* (NGO campaigns, demographic shift) while [[1.1.10 The Development of the Environmental Movement]] provides the *historical examples* (Rachel Carson, Chernobyl, Rio, etc.).

**EVS spectrum → Real-world responses:**
[[1.1.8 Technocentric, Anthropocentric and Ecocentric Worldviews]] underpins analysis across all later topics — any time students evaluate a proposed solution to an environmental problem, they are applying this spectrum.
